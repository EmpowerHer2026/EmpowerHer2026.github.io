The Barriers We Break

Women face real, systemic obstacles every day

📉

Low Workforce Participation

Significantly fewer women join the workforce compared to men, limiting economic independence and family prosperity.

🏫

Early School Dropout

Poverty, household duties, and early marriage force many girls to leave school before completing their education.

🚌

Mobility & Safety Issues

Travelling for education or employment remains unsafe and inaccessible for many women in India.

🔄

Limited Reskilling

Women returning from career breaks have few pathways to learn new, market-relevant skills for modern jobs.

🔌

Skill–Job Mismatch

Existing skills often don't align with available job opportunities, leaving capable women unemployed.

🏛️

Social Norms & Traditions

Deep-rooted cultural norms discourage women from working or pursuing independence outside the household.

Why It Matters

Transforming lives & communities

EmpowerHer's impact goes beyond individual women — it ripples outward into families, communities, and the nation as a whole. Economic independence for women means stronger households, better-educated children, and a more resilient society.

By addressing the root causes of gender inequality in education and employment, EmpowerHer creates systemic, lasting change.
